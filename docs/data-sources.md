# Public data sources

The compact repository does not redistribute the complete working archive. It records the authoritative public source and the role that source played.

| Source | Project role | Public access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **EPA CAMPD** (Clean Air Markets Program Data) | Power-unit emissions, heat input, operating activity and reported control-equipment records used in source-level comparisons | <https://campd.epa.gov/> |
| **EPA AQS AirData** (Air Quality System) | Historical ambient PM2.5, ozone, nitrogen dioxide and sulfur dioxide monitoring observations | <https://aqs.epa.gov/aqsweb/airdata/download_files.html> |
| **NOAA NCEI** Synoptic Summary of the Day v2 and Global Historical Climatology Network Hourly | Weather context, including temperature, precipitation and available wind variables in retained experiments | <https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/oa/synoptic-summary-of-the-day/v2/access/by-year/> and <https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/global-historical-climatology-network-hourly> |
| **DOE AFDC** (Alternative Fuels Data Center) | Transportation laws, incentives and programme descriptions used in the intervention library | <https://afdc.energy.gov/laws> |
| Federal and state agency records | Implementation facts, plan revisions, enforcement context and sector-specific documents | Linked from individual evidence cards where used |
| **original studies** and technical reports | Published methods, comparison designs and externally reported findings used for context or validation | Retain their original citations and terms |

“Policy data” in GreenSeer therefore includes different record types: legal provisions, collected policy documents, reported equipment changes and research evidence. A library entry is not automatically a verified operating intervention. The public `source-manifest.json` is machine-readable and the original agencies remain authoritative.
